# Top Stories

## US 30-year Treasury yield hits highest since 2002 | FT
Insight: The 30-year yield reached its highest level since 2002, extending a long-end steepening that is raising the risk-free discount rate for every asset class. Long-duration equities and rate-sensitive credit carry the most exposure.
Action: Trim duration-heavy growth exposure in ASEAN portfolios; favor shorter-duration value, banks, and dividend payers until the long end stabilizes.
Source: https://www.ft.com/content/us-30-year-treasury-yield-highest-since-2002

## Surging Treasury yields pose a brand new problem for Kevin Warsh and the Fed | CNBC Markets
Insight: Long-end yields are rising while markets expect a firmer Fed stance on inflation, tightening financial conditions without a policy move. This narrows the room for any near-term easing signal.
Action: Position for a higher-for-longer US rate path; avoid assets whose thesis depends on Fed cuts in the next two quarters.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/surging-treasury-yields-are-posing-a-brand-new-problem-for-kevin-warsh-and-the-fed.html

## Global debt tops $365 trillion as economists sound alarm over 'vicious cycle' | CNBC Markets
Insight: Global debt above $365 trillion, combined with higher yields, raises refinancing risk and crowd-out pressure on emerging market issuance, including Indonesia and Malaysia sovereign supply.
Action: Monitor EM sovereign auction demand and currency reserves as a leading indicator for local bond market stress.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/global-debt-tops-365-trillion-as-economists-sound-alarm-over-vicious-cycle.html

## The Fed's main inflation measure will be released Wednesday | CNBC Top
Insight: The PCE release is the week's key macro catalyst. A hot print reinforces the higher-for-longer path and pressures Asian currencies; a soft print gives risk assets temporary relief.
Action: Keep position sizes moderate into the print; use any relief rally to rebalance rather than add risk.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/the-feds-main-inflation-measure-will-be-released-wednesday-heres-what-to-expect.html

## Oil prices fall as crude exports recover at Saudi Arabia's Red Sea ports | CNBC Asia
Insight: Saudi export recovery via the Red Sea port network eased supply fears, pushing crude lower and partly reversing the earlier Hormuz-driven spike. Lower oil is a net positive for Asian importers, including Indonesia and Thailand.
Action: Use oil weakness to reduce hedge costs; energy importers' margins should improve if the recovery holds.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/oil-prices-fall-as-crude-exports-recover-at-saudi-arabias-red-sea-ports.html

## Trump rejects Iran's conditional ceasefire proposal as Saudi coalition intercepts projectiles | CNBC Asia
Insight: The rejection keeps the geopolitical risk premium alive, meaning oil can re-spike on any escalation headline. Tail risk to inflation and to importing economies remains material.
Action: Keep a small energy hedge in place; avoid short-volatility structures in crude.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/trump-rejects-irans-conditional-ceasefire-proposal-wsj-reports.html

## Hedge funds now hold a record share of the $30 trillion Treasury market | CNBC Top
Insight: Record hedge fund concentration in Treasuries increases the probability of sharp, correlated unwinds in a single asset class, which would transmit quickly to global funding markets.
Action: Track repo and volatility metrics; avoid leveraged carry trades that depend on stable Treasury liquidity.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/hedge-funds-now-hold-a-record-share-of-the-30-trillion-treasury-market.html

## US consumer confidence plunges to lowest level since 2014 | The Edge Singapore
Insight: Confidence at a 2014 low, even with a solid economy, signals a demand-side risk ahead. That supports a softer US demand path, relevant for Asian exporters, while capping further yield upside.
Action: Favor domestically driven ASEAN names over export-reliant cyclicals in the near term.
Source: https://www.theedgesingapore.com/news/us/asian-stocks-set-rebound-oil-prices-ease

## BEI Buka Short Selling 1 Oktober, Investor Harus Punya Modal Rp 50 Juta | CNBC Indonesia

# Indonesia Focus

- JCI level and trend: 6,147.86, down -1.51% on the day and -2.06% on the week. Trend is clearly negative and Indonesia is the weakest ASEAN large cap tracked.
- 10Y government bond yield: 7.14%, elevated. With global long-end yields at multi-decade highs, upward pressure on IDR yields persists and raises the sovereign funding cost.
