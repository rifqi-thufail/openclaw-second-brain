## Tooling notes / lessons
- `sessions_spawn` fails with "unknown parent session" from the telegram-direct session; agent-to-agent is restricted. Prefer working in-session.
- `kind: script` automation payloads are headless JS code-mode, NOT bash. For bash use an isolated agentTurn that runs exec, or a wrapper script.
- yfinance drops FX (`=X`) tickers inside a mixed bulk download; fetch FX separately.
- Use `trash` over `rm`; inspect config/schedulers before editing and preserve/merge.
